# Polish political timeline, 2015–July 2026

Last researched: **29 July 2026**.

This is a factual research base for a future DSS Poland outline. It follows the
modern Polish left from the alliance that returned it to parliament in 2019,
through COVID, the formation of Poland 2050, the fall of the PiS government,
the Left's return to cabinet, the Razem split, and the political landscape in
July 2026.

Each entry separates the historical event from its likely **adaptation value**.
Those notes identify useful pressures and forks; they are not claims that the
game must follow one story.

The continuous playable campaign currently adapts a selected route from
October 2019 through July 2023. This chronology is broader than that route:
an event's presence here does not by itself mean that it has been implemented.
The exact playable boundary is recorded in
[the skeleton](PLAYABLE_SKELETON.md).

Counterfactual branches, party successions and reusable crisis chains are kept
in the separate [scenario ledger](SCENARIO_LEDGER.md). The proposed party,
voter, economy and institutional systems are in the
[political simulation model](POLITICAL_MODEL.md). The communications research
is in the [media model](MEDIA_MODEL.md). This file remains the factual
chronology.

## Names that must not be conflated

- **SLD** (*Sojusz Lewicy Demokratycznej*, Democratic Left Alliance) was the
  established post-communist social-democratic party and the legal electoral
  committee used by the united left list in 2019.
- **Wiosna** (Spring) was Robert Biedroń's socially progressive party. It later
  merged with SLD.
- **Razem** (Together) remained a separate party. It cooperated on the 2019 and
  2023 left lists, but did not merge into Nowa Lewica.
- **Lewica** (The Left) can mean the electoral umbrella, parliamentary club, or
  broader political camp depending on date and context.
- **Nowa Lewica** (New Left) is the party created from SLD and Wiosna. Its
  merger was completed politically in 2021.
- **Zjednoczona Lewica** (United Left) is also the proper name of the failed
  2015 electoral coalition. It is a useful informal design label, but should not
  be presented as the official name of the 2019 list.
- **KO** (Civic Coalition) is the PO-led liberal/centrist alliance.
- **PSL** (Polish People's Party) is the agrarian, Christian-democratic and
  centrist party.
- **Polska 2050** was Szymon Hołownia's movement/party. It ran with PSL as
  **Trzecia Droga** (Third Way) in 2023; that alliance ended in 2025.
- **PiS** (Law and Justice) led the governing **United Right** camp until 2023.
- **Konfederacja** is the radical-right alliance. Grzegorz Braun's
  **Konfederacja Korony Polskiej** (KKP, Confederation of the Polish Crown)
  separated from it around the 2025 presidential campaign.

The proposed “SLD old guard” and “SLD new blood” are good game abstractions, but
they were not formal historical factions. By contrast, the SLD and Wiosna wings
were formally protected inside Nowa Lewica from the 2021 merger until the party
removed the dual structure in 2025.

## Timeline

### 2015–2018 — the inherited constitutional and movement landscape

- **November 2015–December 2016 — the Constitutional Tribunal crisis becomes
  systemic.** The outgoing and incoming Sejms disputed judicial appointments;
  President Duda declined to swear in three lawfully nominated judges; the
  government did not publish or implement important Tribunal judgments; and
  new laws altered the court's procedure and leadership. In January 2016, the
  European Commission opened its rule-of-law framework for the first time.
  **Adaptation value:** this is the beginning of a legitimacy split, not a
  conventional contest for control of an undisputed court. Later governments
  inherit rival claims about judges, judgments, publication and who is
  constitutionally entitled to repair the institution.
  ([European Commission recommendation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016H1374);
  [Venice Commission opinion](https://www.venice.coe.int/webforms/documents/?pdf=CDL-AD%282016%29026-e))

- **3–6 October 2016 — Black Monday forces a retreat.** Up to roughly 100,000
  people joined nationwide actions against a near-total abortion ban. Three
  days later the Sejm rejected the citizens' bill **352–58**, with 18
  abstentions.
  **Adaptation value:** the future Women's Strike is born from a demonstrable
  movement victory. Street mobilisation can change governing-party votes, but
  it does not itself produce a liberal abortion law or a permanent alliance
  with any one party.
  ([PAP on the vote](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C665148%2Csejm-odrzucil-obywatelski-projekt-zaostrzajacy-prawo-aborcyjne.html);
  [Amnesty International on the reversal](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/press-release/2016/10/poland-women-force-historic-u-turn-on-proposed-abortion-ban/))

- **July 2017 — judicial protests and Duda's double veto create a presidential
  fault line inside the right.** After demonstrations against a package of
  court laws, Duda vetoed the Supreme Court and National Council of the
  Judiciary bills while signing the ordinary-courts law. He later offered his
  own versions.
  **Adaptation value:** Duda is usually PiS-aligned but not a party-controlled
  automaton. Public pressure, ownership of a reform, rivalry with the justice
  minister, constitutional legacy and expansion of presidential influence can
  all create a temporary opening.
  ([President of Poland](https://www.prezydent.pl/aktualnosci/wydarzenia/prezydent-zdecydowalem-o-zawetowaniu-ustawy-o-sadzie-najwyzszym-i-krs%2C669);
  [European Parliament chronology](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/TA-8-2017-0442_EN.html))

- **17 January and 23 March 2018 — Black Wednesday and Black Friday remobilise
  the abortion-rights movement.** Protests followed the rejection of the
  liberalising *Save Women* proposal and continued work on the restrictive
  *Stop Abortion* bill; the largest March demonstration drew tens of thousands
  in Warsaw.
  **Adaptation value:** mobilisation is a renewable but exhaustible capacity.
  It depends on local organisers, a legible threat and trust that political
  allies will not merely borrow the crowd and abandon the bill.
  ([Amnesty International](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2018/01/poland-nationwide-protests-against-further-restrictions-to-abortion/);
  [PAP on Black Wednesday](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C1249923%2Cczarna-sroda---w-kraju-protesty-ws-zmian-przepisow-dot-aborcji-.html);
  [Polsat News on Black Friday](https://www.polsatnews.pl/wiadomosc/2018-03-23/rozpoczal-sie-czarny-piatek-setki-demonstrantow-na-polskich-ulicach/))

### 2019 — rebuilding a parliamentary left

- **8–27 April — a nationwide teachers' strike closes much of the school
  system.** ZNP and FZZ began an open-ended pay strike during the examination
  season and suspended it shortly before the *matura* exams.
  **Adaptation value:** the future united Left must decide whether it is merely
  sympathetic to organised labour or willing to spend political capital,
  organise a strike fund and accept disruption to defend it. The dispute also
  foreshadows later fights over teachers' pay, curriculum, religion and sex
  education.
  ([PAP on the strike opening](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C432973%2Csolidarity-signs-agreement-govt-two-teachers-unions-go-strike.html);
  [PAP on its suspension](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C442794%2Cteachers-suspend-strike-until-september.html))

- **19–20 July — SLD, Wiosna and Razem unite.** Włodzimierz Czarzasty, Robert
  Biedroń and Adrian Zandberg announced a joint parliamentary list; PPS joined
  the following day. Biedroń framed unity as the lesson of the Left's failure to
  enter the Sejm in 2015.
  **Adaptation value:** a natural opening congress. The player inherits an
  alliance made for electoral survival, not an ideologically or
  organisationally unified party.
  ([PAP, 19 July](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C485767%2Cbiedron-chcemy-odrobic-lekcje-ze-lewica-wygrywa-kiedy-jest-zjednoczona.html);
  [PAP, 20 July](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C486235%2Cpps-przystapilo-do-koalicji-z-sld-wiosna-i-lewica-razem.html))

- **15 August — the alliance chooses the SLD legal vehicle.** Because a party
  name change could not be registered in time, the list formally ran as the SLD
  electoral committee while campaigning under the Lewica logo.
  **Adaptation value:** institutional asymmetry. SLD controls the legal and
  organisational machinery, while Wiosna and Razem supply brands, candidates,
  activists and legitimacy.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C497271%2Clewica-idzie-do-wyborow-pod-szyldem-sld-ze-znakiem-graficznym-lewica.html))

- **13 October — Lewica returns to the Sejm.** The SLD committee won
  **12.56% and 49 seats**. PiS won 43.59% and 235 seats, KO 27.40% and 134,
  PSL 8.55% and 30, and Konfederacja 6.81% and 11.
  **Adaptation value:** unity works, but leaves the player in opposition against
  a narrow PiS majority. The 49-person delegation contains three distinct party
  centres and several smaller traditions.
  ([official PKW result](https://sejmsenat2019.pkw.gov.pl/sejmsenat2019/pl/wyniki/sejm/pl);
  [OSCE/ODIHR final report](https://aceproject.org/ero-en/regions/europe/PL/poland-parliamentary-elections-final-report-2019))

### 2020 — presidential failure, COVID and a new competitor

- **6 January — Razem backs Robert Biedroń.** Razem's national council endorsed
  the Wiosna leader as the common presidential candidate of the parliamentary
  left.
  **Adaptation value:** the first major distribution-of-prestige choice. A
  common candidate preserves unity but ties all partners to Wiosna's campaign.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C566478%2Crazem-poparlo-biedronia-jako-wspolnego-kandydata-lewicy-na-prezydenta.html))

- **13–31 March — Poland closes its borders and enters lockdown.** Schools,
  much retail and public venues closed; entry and movement were restricted;
  and the government declared a state of epidemic. Successive “anti-crisis
  shields” shifted the political argument from whether to intervene to whom the
  state should protect.
  **Adaptation value:** the crisis should open parallel tracks for public-health
  credibility, workers exposed to infection, small firms, civil liberties,
  local-government finance and the capacity of the health service. Those
  choices should later shape vaccine trust and the far right's anti-restriction
  appeal.
  ([border closure](https://www.gov.pl/web/koronawirus/zamykamy-granice-przed-koronawirusem);
  [state of epidemic](https://www.gov.pl/web/koronawirus/wprowadzamy-stan-epidemii-w-polsce);
  [31 March restrictions](https://www.gov.pl/web/mswia/nowe-obostrzenia-w-zwiazku-z-epidemia-koronawirusa))

- **March–6 May — COVID turns the presidential election into a constitutional
  crisis.** The first wave halted ordinary campaigning. PiS pursued an
  all-postal vote for 10 May while opposition parties demanded a postponement.
  Jarosław Kaczyński and Jarosław Gowin finally announced that the vote would
  not take place on that date. A later Supreme Audit Office review found serious
  problems in the preparations.
  **Adaptation value:** an ideal pivotal crisis. Public health, civil liberties,
  electoral legitimacy, campaign visibility, social spending, and relations
  with every opposition partner collide at once.
  ([Polish Radio](https://www.polskieradio.pl/395/7784/artykul/2505619%2Cupdate-poland%E2%80%99s-presidential-elections-deferred-amid-epidemic);
  [NIK audit](https://www.nik.gov.pl/kontrola-panstwowa-2021/05/kontrola-wyborow-korespondencyjnych-prezydenta-rp.html))

- **17 June — eleven candidates meet in the final full-field TVP debate.**
  Every candidate received one minute on each of five common questions:
  refugee relocation, First Communion during school religion lessons,
  same-sex family law, the euro and a future COVID vaccine, followed by a
  one-minute free statement. There were no direct rebuttals. TVP reported a
  mass audience, while ODIHR later found that the public broadcaster had not
  provided balanced and impartial campaign coverage.
  **Adaptation value:** a compact same-turn event hub can recap the real format
  while allowing a Left breakthrough or a rival's dominant clip. Zandberg gets
  a higher performance ceiling: a representative post-debate survey in 2015
  had named him the strongest participant in that leaders' debate.
  ([PAP recap](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C664391%2Cpresidential-candidates-hold-second-debate.html);
  [TVP audience report](https://centruminformacji.tvp.pl/48584981/rekordowa-widownia-debaty-prezydenckiej-w-telewizji-polskiej);
  [OSCE/ODIHR report](https://odihr.osce.org/odihr/464595);
  [2015 Millward Brown result reported by TVN24](https://tvn24.pl/polska/rozmowa-o-polsce-ewy-kopacz-i-beaty-szydlo-ra588259-ls3316106))

- **28 June — the first presidential round marginalises the parliamentary
  left.** Andrzej Duda won 43.50%, Rafał Trzaskowski 30.46%, independent
  Szymon Hołownia 13.87%, Krzysztof Bosak 6.78%, Władysław
  Kosiniak-Kamysz 2.36%, and Biedroń only **2.22%**.
  **Adaptation value:** a severe unity and leadership shock. Hołownia proves
  that a new centrist can rapidly occupy anti-establishment and soft-opposition
  space; Bosak proves the far right has a national presidential base.
  ([OSCE/ODIHR report](https://odihr.osce.org/odihr/464595))

- **12 July — Duda narrowly wins re-election.** Duda defeated Trzaskowski
  **51.03% to 48.97%**.
  **Adaptation value:** the opposition loses by a margin small enough to fuel
  years of blame, tactical-voting arguments and counterfactual coalition
  debates.
  ([official PKW result](https://prezydent20200628.pkw.gov.pl/prezydent20200628/en/wyniki/pl))

- **July–September — Hołownia turns his campaign into Poland 2050.** The
  movement began organising after the election and announced a political party
  in September; the party was formally entered in the court register in April
  2021.
  **Adaptation value:** a new actor with high personal appeal, weak inherited
  structures and a claim to transcend the PO–PiS divide begins competing for
  voters, activists and future coalition leverage.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C683135%2Cindependent-candidates-movement-files-registration.html);
  [Warsaw court register](https://bip.warszawa.so.gov.pl/rejestr-partii/1904/444/polska-2050-szymona-holowni))

- **18–26 September — the “Five for Animals” nearly breaks the United Right.**
  Despite party discipline, Solidarna Polska opposed the animal-welfare bill
  and most Agreement MPs abstained. PiS politicians declared the coalition
  broken before Kaczyński, Ziobro and Gowin negotiated a renewed agreement.
  **Adaptation value:** this is an excellent template for a coalition rupture
  triggered by a cross-cutting issue rather than a grand constitutional
  question. Rural interests, a leader's personal cause, party discipline,
  cabinet offices and opposition votes all matter.
  ([PAP on the rupture](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C719929%2Cgowin-koalicja-istnieje-ale-jestesmy-na-wirazu.html);
  [PAP on the renewed agreement](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C724379%2Cziobro-kolejne-lata-tez-beda-dobrym-czasem-dla-polski-gdy-bedziemy-zgodnie))

- **22 October — the Constitutional Tribunal tightens the abortion ban.** The
  tribunal struck down access based on severe foetal abnormality, provoking
  daily protests led by the Women's Strike during the pandemic. Road blockades,
  workplace and school actions, church protests and a national strike broadened
  the conflict beyond a single Sejm bill. The movement linked abortion to
  health funding, secular schools, Church finance and the legitimacy of the
  Tribunal. The government delayed publication of the judgment until January
  2021 but did not reverse it.
  **Adaptation value:** this is not a one-event popularity bonus. Local
  organisers, legal-aid networks, protest safety, movement trust and
  mobilisation fatigue become durable assets. The Left can amplify the movement
  without owning it, negotiate with it, or alienate it by substituting party
  branding for delivery.
  ([PAP on the fifth day of protests](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C744518%2Cdemonstrators-bring-gridlock-polish-cities-abortion-protests.html);
  [Human Rights Watch on the movement and threats](https://www.hrw.org/news/2021/03/31/poland-escalating-threats-women-activists);
  [European Court of Human Rights case summary](https://www.echr.coe.int/w/judgment-concerning-poland-7))

- **Autumn–December — the second wave exposes the health system and starts the
  vaccine argument.** Mortality peaked sharply in the fourth quarter; the
  government adopted its national vaccination programme on 15 December.
  **Adaptation value:** COVID must have delayed consequences. Choices made
  during the first wave affect hospital overload, trust in experts, willingness
  to vaccinate and whether anti-system actors can make opposition to mandates a
  lasting political identity.
  ([Statistics Poland on 2020 mortality](https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/population/mortality-and-deaths-by-causes-/mortality-and-deaths-by-causes-in-2020%2C1%2C1.html);
  [national vaccination programme](https://www.gov.pl/web/koronawirus/rzad-przyjal-narodowy-program-szczepien))

### 2021 — merger, tactical deals and internal revolt

- **17 March — SLD adopts the Nowa Lewica name.** The party announced that the
  unification congress with Wiosna would take place later in the year.
  **Adaptation value:** changing the logo is easier than merging organisations.
  Appointments, regional structures, lists and control of resources become
  internal bargaining systems.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C834966%2Csld-i-wiosna-pod-nazwa-nowa-lewica-kongres-zjednoczeniowy-odbedzie-sie-w))

- **4 May — Lewica helps pass the EU recovery-fund law.** After negotiating
  conditions with Mateusz Morawiecki, much of the Left backed the legislation
  needed to ratify the EU recovery fund. The government needed opposition votes
  because Solidarna Polska opposed it. KO accused Lewica of rescuing PiS.
  **Adaptation value:** the clearest historical precedent for an issue-by-issue
  Left–PiS deal. It can win policy concessions and national relevance, but
  sharply damages opposition trust and intensifies internal dissent.
  ([Reuters via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy/polish-parliament-approves-legislation-needed-for-eu-recovery-fund-2495108))

- **3 July — Donald Tusk returns to lead PO.** The former prime minister
  re-entered domestic politics as acting Civic Platform chair.
  **Adaptation value:** KO becomes more centralised and more capable of imposing
  a two-bloc frame on the opposition. Smaller partners gain a stronger potential
  prime minister and a more demanding competitor.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C903588%2Ctusk-returns-polish-politics-attack-government.html))

- **July — Czarzasty suspends internal opponents.** Eight Nowa Lewica members,
  including six MPs, were suspended amid a dispute over the merger and the
  planned division into SLD and Wiosna factions.
  **Adaptation value:** the merger can produce a leadership coup, defections or
  a splinter if faction guarantees and list places are handled badly.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C948509%2Cczarzasty-o-sytuacji-nowej-lewicy-konczy-sie-trudny-proces-konsolidacji))

- **11 August — Gowin's Agreement leaves the United Right.** After Jarosław
  Gowin's dismissal, Agreement withdrew from the governing coalition, depriving
  PiS of a stable formal majority.
  **Adaptation value:** opposition parties can attempt a no-confidence path,
  bargain bill by bill, recruit defectors, or wait for an election. A minority
  government makes tactical cooperation with PiS both more valuable and more
  toxic.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C927241%2Cjunior-partner-leaves-polands-ruling-coalition.html))

- **2 September onward — the Belarus border crisis produces a state of
  emergency.** The government described the movement of migrants through
  Belarus as a hybrid operation and imposed emergency restrictions in the
  border zone. Humanitarian access, pushbacks, press access and border security
  divided the opposition.
  **Adaptation value:** migration should not be a single left–right slider.
  Security credibility, asylum law, humanitarian networks, relations with local
  residents and vulnerability to far-right attacks can move independently.
  ([Polish government](https://www.gov.pl/web/primeminister/the-safety-of-poland-comes-first--state-of-emergency-near-the-border-with-belarus);
  [PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C940096%2Cpresident-declares-state-emergency-polands-border-regions.html))

- **9 October — the SLD–Wiosna unification congress completes the merger.**
  Czarzasty and Biedroń became co-chairs, representing the SLD and Wiosna
  components respectively.
  **Adaptation value:** formal dual power stabilises the merger in the short
  term but embeds parallel patronage and veto systems.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C967731%2Cczarzasty-i-biedron-wspolprzewodniczacymi-nowej-lewicy-podali-propozycje))

- **September–November — Izabela Sajbor's death renews abortion protests.**
  The pregnant 30-year-old died of septic shock in a Pszczyna hospital; after
  the case became public, demonstrations under the slogan “Not one more”
  connected the Constitutional Tribunal ruling to fear and delay in clinical
  care.
  **Adaptation value:** abortion law has an implementation track as well as a
  parliamentary one. Prosecutorial guidance, hospital management, conscience
  clauses and doctors' legal confidence can save or cost lives even while a
  bill is blocked.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/ta-tragedia-zyla-cala-polska-wazna-decyzja-sadu-w-sprawie-smierci-izabeli-z-pszczyny);
  [BMJ](https://www.bmj.com/content/375/bmj.n2698))

- **14 December — dissenters form a PPS parliamentary circle.** Former
  Nowa Lewica MPs and Senator Wojciech Konieczny established the PPS group.
  **Adaptation value:** a concrete splinter outcome for failed merger management
  and a reminder that “left unity” always includes organisations outside
  Nowa Lewica.
  ([PPS](https://ppspl.eu/2021/12/14/powstaje-kolo-parlamentarne-pps/))

- **December — the Pegasus and “Lex TVN” conflicts peak.** Independent
  forensic work confirmed that opposition campaign chief Krzysztof Brejza had
  been infected with Pegasus spyware. On 27 December Duda vetoed a media
  ownership law widely understood to threaten US-owned TVN.
  **Adaptation value:** surveillance evidence can launch investigations,
  resignations and election-legitimacy disputes; the media veto shows a
  PiS-aligned president can still defect when constitutional reputation and a
  strategic foreign relationship are at stake.
  ([Amnesty International](https://securitylab.amnesty.org/latest/2022/01/poland-use-of-pegasus-spyware-to-hack-politicians-highlights-threat-to-civil-society/);
  [presidential veto](https://www.prezydent.pl/kancelaria/archiwum/andrzej-duda/aktualnosci/wydarzenia/prezydent-zawetowal-ustawy-o-radiofonii-i-telewizji%2C47225))

### 2022 — war changes the policy axis

- **January–July — the Polish Deal tax reform requires emergency repair.**
  Large tax and contribution changes entered into force amid confusion over
  pay, payroll systems and who gained or lost; the government substantially
  revised the reform during the year.
  **Adaptation value:** a flagship redistributive reform can fail through
  administrative capacity rather than ideology. The Left can defend its
  progressive elements, attack the chaos, offer technical repair, or let the
  government own the failure.
  ([Ministry of Finance retrospective](https://www.gov.pl/web/finanse/rozliczenie-pit-zwroty-i-doplaty-podatku-za-2022-r))

- **24 February onward — Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine transforms
  Polish politics.** Poland received millions of border crossings and became a
  central logistical and political supporter of Ukraine. The major parties
  broadly agreed on supporting Ukraine, but disputes remained over refugees,
  defence, energy, inflation and the war's duration. Razem strongly backed
  Ukrainian self-defence and called for solidarity across the regional left.
  **Adaptation value:** foreign and security policy can no longer be a flavour
  stat. It cross-cuts the Polish left between Atlanticist/regional-solidarity
  positions, pacifism, social spending, refugee integration and scepticism of
  militarisation.
  ([Razem statement](https://partiarazem.pl/stanowiska/2022/03/08/left-solidarity-with-ukraine);
  [European Journal of Political Research political-data review](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/2047-8852.12419))

- **17 June — the Council approves Poland's KPO, but payment remains
  conditional.** EU finance ministers approved the Commission's positive
  assessment of Poland's recovery and resilience plan. The decision did not
  release the money automatically: disbursement remained tied to milestones
  and targets, including reform of the disciplinary regime for judges.
  **Adaptation value:** a KPO event needs separate approval, milestone and
  delivery states. The Left can defend the investments while auditing whether
  its 2021 bargain or the government's judicial changes actually satisfy the
  conditions.
  ([Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2022/06/17/recovery-fund-ministers-welcome-assessment-of-poland-s-national-plan/))

- **August — the Oder ecological disaster becomes a state-capacity scandal.**
  Mass fish deaths and severe pollution spread along the river while Polish and
  German authorities searched for the cause and criticised the speed of
  warning and response.
  **Adaptation value:** a regional environmental event can connect climate,
  industrial regulation, local government, cross-border relations and minister
  competence. Early transparency should matter more than a simple spending
  choice.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C1399834%2Cno-toxic-substances-found-water-samples-from-odra-says-climate-minister.html))

- **15 October — Sławomir Mentzen succeeds Janusz Korwin-Mikke as leader of
  KORWiN.** Korwin-Mikke retained a founder's role, while party delegates
  elected Mentzen, the outgoing leader's chosen successor. This changed the
  public face of Konfederacja's libertarian current without dissolving its
  nationalist and Braunist partners.
  **Adaptation value:** succession can professionalise the tax-revolt message
  while shifting resources among three internal right-wing currents rather
  than moving Konfederacja as one unit.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C1453717%2Cjanusz-korwin-mikke-ustapil-z-funkcji-prezesa-partii-korwin-wybrano-jego))

- **15 November — a missile kills two people in Przewodów.** Polish and allied
  assessments indicated that the missile was probably fired by Ukrainian air
  defence during a mass Russian attack.
  **Adaptation value:** this is a short, high-risk crisis chain for verification,
  NATO consultation, public communication and escalation control. Relations
  built with Washington, Kyiv, the military and the president determine which
  information the player can trust.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/news%2C1481600%2Cpresident-says-missile-was-probably-ukrainian.html))

- **Throughout the year — the opposition debates one list versus several.**
  Tusk promoted the logic of a single anti-PiS bloc, while the Left, PSL,
  Poland 2050 and their electorates weighed identity against electoral-system
  advantages.
  **Adaptation value:** this is not merely a coalition choice after an election.
  Pre-election list construction changes thresholds, campaign visibility,
  candidate places, faction dissent and who can claim leadership.
  ([CBOS analysis](https://cbos.pl/EN/publications/reports/open_file.php?id=6524))

### 2023 — victory, coalition and the return to government

- **13 February — the Wolnościowcy rupture removes three MPs from
  Konfederacja's parliamentary circle.** Artur Dziambor had been expelled, and
  Dobromir Sośnierz and Jakub Kulesza then announced their departure.
  Wolnościowcy planned a separate Sejm circle after disputes over cooperation
  and candidate-selection primaries.
  **Adaptation value:** the split can weaken Konfederacja's parliamentary
  resources while leaving Mentzen, the nationalists and Braunists less
  constrained inside the remaining federation.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C1535506%2Cw-sejmie-postanie-kolo-wolnosciowcow-powodem-tarcia-w-konfederacji.html))

- **27 February — the left parties renew electoral cooperation.** Nowa Lewica,
  Razem, PPS and Unia Pracy signed an agreement to run together.
  **Adaptation value:** unity has been rebuilt after the 2021 split, but Razem
  remains organisationally independent and can later distinguish parliamentary
  support from cabinet participation.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/news%2C1542326%2Cnowa-lewica-razem-pps-i-unia-pracy-podpisaly-porozumienie-o-wspolpracy-w))

- **27 April — PSL and Poland 2050 create Third Way.** The parties agreed to run
  together as a centrist alternative to both PiS and KO.
  **Adaptation value:** Hołownia's movement gains local organisation and PSL
  gains a fresher national brand. The alliance can be partner, competitor, or
  electoral-threshold casualty.
  ([Poland 2050](https://polska2050.pl/umowa-koalicyjna-z-polskim-stronnictwem-ludowym/))

- **14 May — PiS announces that 500+ will become 800+ from 2024.** Kaczyński
  used a party programme convention to promise an increase of the universal
  child benefit to 800 złoty while retaining its existing eligibility rules.
  **Adaptation value:** PiS social solidarism can answer both inflation and
  Konfederacja's market radicalism. The Left must decide whether to welcome a
  larger universal benefit, contest its timing and real value, or attach public
  services and wage policy without letting PiS monopolise social security.
  ([Ministry of Family and Social Policy](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/wyzsze-swiadczenie-wychowawcze-juz-od-przyszlego-roku))

- **May–4 June — “Lex Tusk” helps trigger a mass opposition march.** A law
  creating an administrative commission on Russian influence drew warnings
  that it could exclude political opponents from office. Tusk's 4 June march
  became a major demonstration of opposition mobilisation.
  **Adaptation value:** an attempt to disable a rival can instead unify the
  opposition. The Left must choose between a common democratic front, its own
  contingent and fear of disappearing into KO's leadership spectacle.
  ([European Parliament briefing](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/RegData/etudes/BRIE/2023/749765/EPRS_BRI%282023%29749765_EN.pdf);
  [Euronews on the march and law](https://www.euronews.com/2023/06/04/the-polish-opposition-is-troubled-by-a-new-russian-influence-law-what-is-it-really-about))

- **13–14 June — Dorota's death brings “Not one more” protests back to the
  streets.** Demonstrations in roughly 40 towns and cities followed the death
  from septic shock of a pregnant woman in Nowy Targ after her waters broke in
  the fifth month.
  **Adaptation value:** movement mobilisation can return through a medical
  tragedy even after the peak protest wave subsides. The immediate policy
  battlefield is hospital protocol, doctors' fear and health-ministry guidance;
  reducing the event to a promise of future legislation wastes the opening and
  movement trust.
  ([Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2023/06/15/not-one-more-thousands-protest-abortion-law-after-latest-death-of-pregnant-woman-in-poland/))

- **April–September — Ukrainian grain divides allies and energises rural
  politics.** Poland first restricted agricultural imports and then continued
  a national embargo after the EU measure expired, producing a sharp dispute
  with Kyiv.
  **Adaptation value:** PSL, agrarian unions and rural Left organisers can force
  foreign-policy idealism to confront farm incomes. Compensation, transit
  controls, confrontation with Brussels and confrontation with Kyiv produce
  different coalitions.
  ([Polish government resolution](https://www.gov.pl/web/premier/uchwala-ws-przedluzenia-srodkow-zapobiegawczych-dotyczacych-przywozu-z-ukrainy-niektorych-produktow);
  [PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/polish-pm-warns-ukraine-over-escalation-of-grain-import-conflict))

- **September — the cash-for-visas affair damages PiS's migration message.**
  Allegations of irregularities and corruption in the visa system forced
  resignations and an international demand for explanations during the
  election campaign.
  **Adaptation value:** migration rhetoric can be reversed by an administrative
  scandal. Investigators, whistle-blowers and consular capacity create a
  corruption chain separate from the border-policy argument.
  ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/3c74c2a95909c19b7297eab4a7c5f7ea))

- **15 October — the democratic opposition wins a Sejm majority.** PiS remained
  the largest list with **35.38% and 194 seats**, but KO won **30.70% and
  157**, Third Way **14.40% and 65**, Nowa Lewica **8.61% and 26**, and
  Konfederacja **7.16% and 18**. KO, Third Way and the Left together held
  248 seats.
  **Adaptation value:** victory comes with a warning: the Left loses almost half
  its 2019 delegation and enters government as the smallest coalition bloc.
  Its 26 seats are pivotal to the majority but insufficient to dictate terms.
  ([official PKW result](https://sejmsenat2023.pkw.gov.pl/sejmsenat2023/pl/sejm/wynik/pl);
  [PAP seat totals](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/sa-oficjalne-wyniki-wyborow))

- **10 November — KO, Third Way and Nowa Lewica sign a coalition agreement.**
  The agreement covered rule of law, public services, social policy, abortion
  access language and coalition procedures.
  **Adaptation value:** the player moves from broad opposition unity to daily
  multi-party bargaining. Ministries, the coalition council, legislative
  sequence and presidential veto strategy become core systems.
  ([coalition agreement](https://platforma.org/dokumenty/umowa-koalicyjna))

- **14 November — Duda's camp floats a post-party legacy rather than a return
  to PiS organisation.** Presidential chief of staff Marcin Mastalerek said he
  could not imagine Duda becoming a party activist or leader of the right
  after his term, while expecting the right to compete for his legacy.
  **Adaptation value:** presidential independence needs more than disagreement
  with Kaczyński. Duda can retain symbolic authority, advisers, foreign
  contacts and a media profile while lacking local branches, deputies and a
  party treasury. A later leadership bid must build or borrow that machine.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/mastalerek-nie-wyobrazam-sobie-zeby-prezydent-duda-po-zakonczeniu-kadencji-byl))

- **November — Razem refuses cabinet posts.** Razem argued that the agreement
  lacked sufficiently firm commitments and funding for its social programme.
  It remained in the Lewica parliamentary club and supported the creation of
  the new government without joining it.
  **Adaptation value:** a historically grounded “confidence and supply from the
  left” branch. Razem can retain leverage and ideological distance, but the two
  sides will compete to claim achievements and assign blame.
  ([Rzeczpospolita](https://www.rp.pl/polityka/art39387721-dlaczego-partia-razem-nie-wejdzie-do-rzadu-tuska-adrian-zandberg-tlumaczy))

- **13 December — Donald Tusk's government is sworn in.** Nowa Lewica received
  the deputy premiership/digital ministry for Krzysztof Gawkowski, the family
  and labour ministry for Agnieszka Dziemianowicz-Bąk, the science ministry for
  Dariusz Wieczorek and the equality portfolio for Katarzyna Kotula.
  **Adaptation value:** the first national executive participation by the Polish
  left since 2005 creates deliverables, scandals, budget fights and collective
  responsibility for compromises made elsewhere in government.
  ([Polish government](https://www.gov.pl/web/primeminister/president-andrzej-duda-appoints-prime-minister-donald-tusk-and-a-new-council-of-ministers))

- **12–27 December — Braun's menorah attack and the public-media takeover open
  two immediate crises.** Braun used a fire extinguisher on a Hanukkah menorah
  in the Sejm and was excluded from the sitting. Days later, the new majority
  replaced public-media management; PiS politicians occupied media buildings
  and Duda challenged the legal method.
  **Adaptation value:** the first event tests whether Konfederacja isolates an
  extremist or lets him build a martyr brand. The second forces the governing
  Left to choose between rapid institutional seizure, slower legislation that
  Duda can veto, and accepting hostile media during the transition.
  ([Sejm committee statement on Braun](https://www.sejm.gov.pl/Sejm10.nsf/biuletyn.xsp?skrnr=MNE-2);
  [public-media crisis](https://www.polskieradio.pl/395/7784/artykul/3305918%2Cstorm-over-public-media-i-the-governments-position))

### 2024 — weak elections, partial achievements and the Razem break

- **Early January — an advertiser backlash tests TV Republika's new role.**
  Several large companies withdrew advertising after offensive comments about
  migrants aired as the station absorbed conservative viewers leaving the
  reorganised public broadcaster.
  **Adaptation value:** commercial sanction can reduce revenue without
  destroying an ideological outlet. Boycott, donations and a grievance
  narrative can reinforce one another; government pressure on advertisers
  would make the station's victim claim stronger.
  ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/1df312136f8972999161a2199993022f))

- **9 January — police arrest Kamiński and Wąsik inside the Presidential
  Palace.** The status of the convicted former CBA chiefs, Duda's earlier
  pardons and their parliamentary mandates produced an institutional conflict
  among the presidency, courts, Sejm and government.
  **Adaptation value:** “settling accounts” with the previous government should
  depend on court legitimacy, procedural restraint and public patience. A legal
  victory can still strengthen an opposition martyr narrative.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/kgp-o-zatrzymaniu-kaminskiego-i-wasika-wszystkie-podjete-czynnosci-byly-zgodne-z-0))

- **1 February — Krzysztof Stanowski launches Kanał Zero.** The
  personality-led YouTube venture mixed long interviews, commentary,
  entertainment and current affairs rather than adopting the structure of a
  traditional party-political newsroom.
  **Adaptation value:** an interviewer can become an agenda-setting political
  institution without endorsing a party. Access, preparation, clip performance
  and the host's judgment matter independently; refusing an invitation does not
  prevent the channel from defining the absence.
  ([Reuters Institute, Digital News Report 2025](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2025/poland))

- **February–March — nationwide farmer protests confront the new government.**
  Protesters opposed Ukrainian agricultural imports and parts of the EU Green
  Deal; the largest Warsaw action ended in clashes with police and no immediate
  settlement followed talks with Tusk.
  **Adaptation value:** PSL's rural credentials become a coalition-stability
  resource. The Left can defend climate goals, side with labouring farmers,
  expose large agribusiness, or concede controls—but cannot satisfy every camp
  at once.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/no-agreement-reached-after-pms-meeting-with-farmers);
  [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/68e62bb822ce6a09eefefe9b2909c023))

- **6 March — the Sejm declares that the Constitutional Tribunal crisis must
  be repaired.** The majority's resolution rejected the legality of the three
  “double judges” and challenged Julia Przyłębska's presidency. The Tribunal
  later ruled the resolution unconstitutional, while the government disputed
  the authority of panels containing defectively appointed judges and stopped
  publishing their decisions.
  **Adaptation value:** a court can issue an adverse judgment that the
  government does not recognise as a valid judgment. Compliance, publication,
  EU-law consequences and public legitimacy must be separate variables; a
  simple “court blocks bill” event cannot represent this crisis.
  ([Sejm resolution](https://eli.sejm.gov.pl/eli/MP/2024/198/ogl/pol);
  [European Court of Human Rights on a defectively composed panel](https://hudoc.echr.coe.int/eng/?i=001-210065))

- **29 March — Duda vetoes over-the-counter emergency contraception for people
  aged 15 and older.** The government answered with a pharmacist-prescription
  pilot rather than abandoning the policy.
  **Adaptation value:** a useful small-scale presidential-blockade template:
  accept defeat, narrow a measure, create an administrative workaround, or
  escalate into a larger reproductive-rights confrontation.
  ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/fe41d1c97ea3e0151d67278e67c0512a))

- **7 April — local elections expose the Left's organisational weakness.**
  Lewica won **6.32%** in the regional-assembly vote and only eight of 552
  regional seats.
  **Adaptation value:** government office does not automatically rebuild local
  structures. Poor local results raise leadership dissent and dependence on KO
  in mayoral and regional deals.
  ([PAP local-government service](https://samorzad.pap.pl/kategoria/aktualnosci/wyniki-wyborow-samorzadowych-2024-sejmiki-wojewodztw-kompletne-dane-pkw))

- **12 April — four abortion bills survive first reading.** The Sejm sent
  proposals from coalition parties to committee instead of rejecting them at
  the outset.
  **Adaptation value:** an apparent coalition advance, but the four separate
  bills reveal different endpoints and tempos inside the governing majority.
  ([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/434b83b5ad0d45d7aa1a6644e025a341))

- **April–29 May — EU recovery money and the end of the Article 7 procedure
  reward the change of government.** The Commission authorised the first KPO
  payment and later withdrew the rule-of-law proposal opened against Poland in
  2017.
  **Adaptation value:** EU credibility converts into money and legitimacy, but
  milestones also constrain budgets and labour regulation. A government can
  claim normalisation while still fighting Duda and disputed domestic judicial
  institutions.
  ([European Commission payment decision](https://commission.europa.eu/document/download/993e281e-4ab7-4d08-8278-71fdb9e3d48a_en?filename=C_2024_2369_1_EN_ACT_part1_v5.pdf);
  [Council Article 7 timeline](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/article-7-procedures/timeline-the-story-of-article-7/))

- **1 June — the public IVF programme begins.** The government restored state
  funding, with PLN 2.5 billion planned through 2028.
  **Adaptation value:** a tangible progressive achievement the Left can claim
  inside coalition, although it is less polarising than abortion reform.
  ([Ministry of Health](https://www.gov.pl/web/zdrowie/zalozenia-programu-in-vitro))

- **28 May–31 August — a soldier's death hardens the Belarus-border
  consensus.** Mateusz Sitek was stabbed through the border barrier on 28 May
  and died on 6 June. A separate controversy over soldiers detained after
  firing warning shots sharpened demands for clearer protection and rules of
  engagement. Parliament passed a law widening legal protection for soldiers,
  police and Border Guard officers in specified emergency uses of force. In
  July CBOS found **73%** opposed allowing people at that border to apply for
  asylum and **84%** supported wider permissibility for officers' use of
  weapons.
  **Adaptation value:** public opinion can move rapidly after a death and after
  political actors reframe a humanitarian question as protection of uniformed
  services. The player must distinguish lawful force, officer safety, asylum
  access, Belarusian hybrid tactics and the dignity of individual refugees
  while operating in a strongly adverse opinion climate.
  ([Ministry of Defence memorial](https://www.gov.pl/web/obrona-narodowa/pamieci-sierzanta-mateusza-sitka);
  [the 26 July act](https://eli.gov.pl/eli/DU/2024/1248/ogl/pol);
  [CBOS border survey](https://www.cbos.pl/EN/publications/reports_text.php?id=6862))

- **9 June — European elections strengthen the radical right.** KO narrowly
  led PiS, while Konfederacja won about **12.1% and six seats**. Lewica won
  about **6.3% and three seats**, only slightly behind Third Way.
  **Adaptation value:** another weak result for the governing Left and a major
  warning that opposition to PiS does not contain the harder right.
  ([official European Parliament results](https://results.elections.europa.eu/en/national-results/poland/2024-2029/))

- **12 July — the coalition loses the abortion decriminalisation vote.** A
  Lewica bill to decriminalise assistance in obtaining an abortion failed
  **215–218**; 24 PSL MPs voted against it.
  **Adaptation value:** the clearest coalition veto-point event. Staying loyal,
  attacking PSL, accepting a narrower compromise, or threatening the coalition
  all carry different social-policy and government-stability costs.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/koalicja-rzadzaca-przegrala-glosowanie-w-sprawie-aborcji-kto-jak-glosowal))

- **9 August — President Duda signs the widow's pension law.** The
  long-campaigned-for Lewica policy allowed eligible widowed people to combine
  part of a survivor's pension with their own benefit.
  **Adaptation value:** a flagship distributive achievement that strengthens
  the labour/social-policy wing and demonstrates why remaining in coalition can
  pay.
  ([Ministry of Family, Labour and Social Policy](https://www.gov.pl/web/family/widows-pension-with-the-presidents-signature-restoring-dignity-to-widows-and-widowers))

- **September — catastrophic flooding hits south-western Poland.** A state of
  natural disaster eventually covered 231 municipalities and the cabinet faced
  rescue, communication, compensation and reconstruction decisions.
  **Adaptation value:** crisis competence should be accumulated before the
  water rises. Local-government relations, emergency services, climate
  adaptation and ministerial competence determine damage and blame.
  ([Statistics Poland](https://stat.gov.pl/en/topics/other-studies/cities-voivodship/activities-of-local-government-units-related-to-the-occurrence-of-floods-in-part-of-the-countrys-territory-in-september-2024%2C24%2C1.html?pdf=1);
  [government briefing](https://www.gov.pl/web/primeminister/parliamentary-briefing-on-the-measures-taken-with-regard-to-the-flood))

- **September — support for receiving Ukrainian refugees falls to a bare
  majority.** In CBOS's series, support declined from **94% in March 2022 to
  53% in September 2024**, while opposition increased from **3% to 40%**.
  **Adaptation value:** solidarity, war fatigue, benefit competition,
  anti-Ukrainian politics and racism must be distinguished. A policy-opinion
  shift is not itself proof of racial hostility, but it enlarges the audience
  available to politicians who turn Ukrainians into a collective threat.
  Housing, school capacity, labour enforcement and honest benefit rules can
  change the trajectory.
  ([CBOS, *Polish Public Opinion*, October 2024](https://cbos.pl/PL/publikacje/public_opinion/2024/10_2024.pdf))

- **7 October — Duda sends the Tribunal-repair laws to the disputed Tribunal
  itself.** Instead of signing two September acts, the president requested
  preventive review, arguing that the package endangered acquired rights and
  legal security. The court did not resolve the applications until July 2025.
  **Adaptation value:** presidential referral can freeze a reform without the
  political clarity of a veto. The player must decide whether to wait, draft a
  narrower law, pursue constitutional amendment, allow contested terms to
  expire, or use administrative non-cooperation at a growing legitimacy cost.
  ([President's Office](https://k.prezydent.pl/kancelaria/aktywnosc-ministrow/szef-kprp-o-wnioskach-prezydenta-rp-do-trybunalu-konstytucyjnego%2C92537);
  [Venice Commission opinion on the package](https://www.venice.coe.int/webforms/documents/?pdf=CDL-AD%282024%29035-e))

- **12 October — Tusk announces a tougher migration strategy.** The prime
  minister proposed temporary territorial suspension of asylum applications,
  alarming politicians in Nowa Lewica and Poland 2050.
  **Adaptation value:** migration becomes an internal coalition and left-faction
  issue, not just a contest with PiS and Konfederacja.
  ([Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2024/10/12/poland-to-suspend-asylum-rights-as-part-of-tougher-migration-policy-to-regain-control-of-borders/))

- **24–27 October — Razem splits, then leaves the Lewica club.** Five
  parliamentarians, including Senate deputy speaker Magdalena Biejat, left Razem
  because they wanted to remain closer to the governing left. Days later,
  Razem's remaining MPs left the Lewica parliamentary club and formed a separate
  circle, arguing that the government was not delivering sufficiently on
  housing, health and social policy.
  **Adaptation value:** the main delayed consequence of Razem staying outside
  cabinet. The pro-cooperation minority defects to the government camp; the
  remaining party gains clarity but loses people and direct influence.
  ([PAP on the five departures](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/rozlam-w-partii-razem-piec-parlamentarzystek-odchodzi);
  [PAP on leaving the club](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/partia-razem-zdecydowala-o-opuszczeniu-klubu-lewicy-i-utworzeniu-kola))

- **23 November — KO chooses Trzaskowski in a presidential primary.**
  Trzaskowski defeated Sikorski **74.75–25.25**.
  **Adaptation value:** the primary creates a real KO leadership fork and gives
  the losing security/foreign-policy current a measured internal base.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/en/news/polands-main-ruling-party-picks-trzaskowski-presidential-candidate))

- **15 December — Nowa Lewica nominates Magdalena Biejat for president.**
  **Adaptation value:** choosing an ex-Razem politician is an attempt to bridge
  the left after the split, but also gives Razem room to field its own candidate
  and contest ownership of the economic left.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/ona-powalczy-o-prezydenture-nowa-lewica-podala-nazwisko-kandydatki))

- **24 December — a Left-led compromise creates a free Christmas Eve.** The
  law made Christmas Eve a statutory day off from 2025 while adding three
  trading Sundays in December.
  **Adaptation value:** a visible workers' gain can require a business and
  coalition compromise that neither side considers complete.
  ([Ministry of Family, Labour and Social Policy](https://www.gov.pl/web/rodzina/wolna-wigilia-od-przyszlego-roku))

### 2025 — a divided-left election and a hostile presidency

- **11 January — Razem nominates Adrian Zandberg.** The presidential election
  now has separate government-left and opposition-left candidates.
  **Adaptation value:** a direct test of whether voters reward access to office
  or programmatic opposition.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/adrian-zandberg-kandydatem-partii-razem-na-prezydenta))

- **16–17 January — Grzegorz Braun challenges Konfederacja's Sławomir
  Mentzen.**
  Braun announced a separate presidential run and was removed from
  Konfederacja's leadership, consolidating KKP as a rival radical-right pole.
  **Adaptation value:** “the far right” must be modelled as competitive camps.
  Mentzen can normalise and expand Konfederacja while Braun radicalises a
  separate electorate.
  ([PAP](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/konfederacja-zwolala-kongres-z-rady-liderow-znikaja-nazwiska-dzialaczy-ugrupowania-brauna))

- **17 January–1 September — the government reduces school religion to one
  lesson and launches optional health education.** The religion change drew
  objections from the Catholic hierarchy. Health education included mental
  health, prevention and sexual health, but the cabinet retreated from making
  it compulsory.
  **Adaptation value:** the school culture war has at least four actors—the
  ministry, teachers, parents/pupils and churches—and two distinct questions:
  curriculum content and whether participation is compulsory. Compromise can
  demobilise supporters without ending conservative opposition.
  ([religion regulation](https://www.gov.pl/web/edukacja/rozporzadzenie-w-sprawie-organizacji-lekcji-religii-podpisane);
  [health-education design](https://www.gov.pl/web/edukacja/edukacja-zdrowotna-w-szkolach-od-2025-roku-ruszaja-prace-przygotowawcze);
  [Ombudsman for Children on opt-out](https://brpd.gov.pl/2025/02/10/edukacja-zdrowotna-nieobowiazkowa-po-zlozeniu-rezygnacji-men-odpowiada-rpd/))

- **21 February–26 March — parliament and President Duda enable temporary
  asylum suspension.** The Sejm passed the government's border bill with only
  38 MPs—mainly from Lewica and Razem—opposed; Duda signed it in March.
  **Adaptation value:** Nowa Lewica can differentiate itself from its own
  government on a rights issue without immediately leaving coalition, while
  Razem can claim consistency from opposition.
  ([Notes from Poland](https://notesfrompoland.com/2025/02/21/polish-parliament-approves-bill-allowing-suspension-of-asylum-rights/);
  [Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/5bc3bfc8fb5dbfcb2ad15e8d7ebaf824))

- **4 March — the Supreme Court simplifies legal gender recognition.** The
  Civil Chamber held that rectification should proceed non-contentiously rather
  than by requiring a trans person to sue their parents.
  **Adaptation value:** trans policy should include everyday legal procedure,
  not only symbolic representation. A judicial route can deliver change but
  invites a later constitutional-court, presidential or parliamentary
  counterattack.
  ([Commissioner for Human Rights](https://bip.brpo.gov.pl/pl/content/rpo-pg-osoby-transplciowe-uzgodnienie-plci-pozywanie-dzieci-malzonka-sn-uchwala))

- **28 April onward — the labour ministry opens a shorter-working-time pilot.**
  Employers and public institutions could apply to test reduced hours without
  reducing pay, with trials beginning in 2026.
  **Adaptation value:** an evidence-producing pilot can build a later universal
  reform, be captured as a subsidy, or be buried after a ministerial change.

## Snapshot at the research cutoff

One **CBOS poll conducted 6–8 July 2026** put KO at 25.1%, PiS at 19.1%,
Konfederacja at 15.2%, KKP at 8.2%, Nowa Lewica at 5.3%, Razem at 4.1%,
PSL at 3.6%, Poland 2050 at 1.1%, and Centrum at 0.1%; 15.7% were undecided.
This is a dated pressure indicator, not a stable forecast or starting balance.
([PAP/CBOS](https://www.pap.pl/aktualnosci/lipcowy-uklad-sil-nowy-sondaz-poparcia-partii-politycznych))

The strategic picture at 29 July is therefore:

- Nowa Lewica is in government, holds the Sejm Marshal's office and can point to
  policies such as IVF restoration and the widow's pension, but remains the
  smallest original coalition bloc.
- Razem is an opposition-left competitor with evidence of an independent
  presidential electorate, while renewed cooperation is again being discussed.
- KO remains the necessary senior partner for the actual governing route.
- PSL is both an indispensable coalition bridge and a veto player on abortion
  and some equality policy.
- Hołownia's once-rising project has fragmented into Poland 2050 and Unia
  Centrum, both facing existential electoral-threshold questions.
- Konfederacja and KKP occupy two different radical-right spaces.
- PiS itself is entering a major Kaczyński–Morawiecki rupture.
- President Nawrocki can block coalition legislation that the government lacks
  the votes to pass over a veto.

## Coalition paths supported by the history

These are constraints and precedents for later design, not a complete game
outline.

| Path | Historical evidence | Principal pressure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **KO + Left + PSL + Poland 2050** | The actual government formed in December 2023 | The Left has pivotal seats but is the smallest bloc; PSL can veto social reform |
| **KO + Left** | Close programmatic cooperation exists, but their 2023 seats total only 183 | Requires a different election, defectors, or outside support |
| **KO + Left + one centrist partner** | Plausible under alternate seat totals; Third Way's later split makes the identity of that partner variable | Cabinet allocation and electoral-list bargaining |
| **Minority KO/Left government tolerated by Razem** | Razem supported creation of the Tusk government while refusing cabinet posts | Budgets and social-policy milestones must retain outside-left support |
| **Issue deal with PiS** | The 2021 recovery-fund vote is the key precedent | Can deliver a policy while causing opposition and internal-left backlash |
| **Formal Left–PiS coalition** | No modern precedent in this period | Would require an exceptional crisis and should carry extreme dissent and legitimacy costs |
| **PSL–Left bargaining** | Widow's pension and the 2026 partnership compromise show cooperation; the 2024 abortion vote shows a hard limit | Social policy can advance incrementally or collapse on values |

Under the actual 2023 seat totals, no two-party combination involving the
26-seat Left reaches the 231-seat majority threshold. Alternate two-party
governments therefore require the game to produce substantially different
election results, not just a post-election dialogue choice.

## Systems suggested by the record

The timeline supports the following simulation tracks:

- **Left unity:** cooperation, common lists and re-merger talks versus separate
  identities and the electoral threshold.
- **Organisational balance:** SLD machinery, Wiosna's social-progressive brand,
  Razem's programmatic independence, parliamentary officeholders, local
  structures and smaller allies.
- **Government delivery:** ministries, budgets, scandals, flagship laws and the
  cost of collective responsibility.
- **Coalition trust:** separate relationships with KO, PSL, Poland 2050/its
  successors, Razem and—only in exceptional or issue-specific cases—PiS.
- **Presidential blockade:** negotiate, mobilise, narrow a bill, postpone it, or
  spend political capital on an expected veto.
- **Constitutional legitimacy:** Tribunal composition, KRS appointments,
  Supreme Court chambers, prosecutors, presidential cooperation, European
  judgments and ordinary case backlog cannot be collapsed into one repair bar.
- **Social mobilisation:** abortion, equality, church–state relations, housing,
  labour, health and pensions draw on different activists and electorates.
- **Security and borders:** Ukraine, defence spending, refugees, Belarus-border
  policy and civil liberties cut across a simple left/right scale.
- **Minority safety and racism:** prejudice, elite rhetoric, discrimination,
  hate incidents, reporting trust and organised violence move differently by
  target group.
- **Media capacity:** public-media legality, Republika's reach, creator-led
  politics, local journalism and the Left's weak communications infrastructure
  form a costly, long-term contest for attention and trust.
- **Right-wing competition:** PiS, Konfederacja, KKP and the Morawiecki camp
  should be able to split, cooperate and cannibalise one another.
- **Election type:** parliamentary, presidential, local, European and Senate
  races use different coalitions, personalities and success conditions.
- **Time-limited crises:** COVID, the abandoned May 2020 election, war,
  inflation and constitutional confrontations can temporarily reorder every
  other priority.

## Research limits and next pass

This is a national high-politics timeline. Before scene writing, a second pass
should add:

- month-by-month COVID restrictions, economic packages and public-health
  controversies;
- major polling series rather than isolated polls;
- named internal actors, regional organisations and leadership contests inside
  SLD/Nowa Lewica, Wiosna and Razem;
- trade unions, feminist and LGBT organisations, housing movements, the
  Catholic Church, public media and business interests;
- cabinet scandals and ministerial performance after 2023;
- budgets, inflation, energy, coal transition and EU recovery-fund milestones;
- exact Sejm votes for each possible coalition-breaking issue;
- local and European election candidate conflicts;
- primary Polish documents for events currently supported by secondary English
  reporting.

Those additions should be recorded in a structured event ledger—date, actors,
preconditions, decision, immediate effect, delayed effect and sources—before
being converted into `.scene.dry` files.
