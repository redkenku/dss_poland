# Event image credits

The event illustrations in `out/html/img/poland/events/` are resized WebP
derivatives of the Wikimedia Commons sources below. They document the event or
provide a closely related setting; none is presented as a photograph of a
counterfactual outcome. No generative image tools were used.

Counterfactual events — the 2026 and 2027 branches, and any outcome the game
invents — carry a photograph of the real institution, place or office-holder the
event takes place in: the Presidential Palace for a veto, the Sejm chamber for a
vote, a party leader for a party decision. The photograph documents that
setting and nothing about how the scene resolves.

Copyright and license terms remain with each source image and are not replaced
by the repository's MIT license. Source and license metadata was checked on
9 August 2026, and the two `aleksander-kwasniewski-*` entries plus the eighteen
illustrations added on 15 August 2026 (`finance-ministry*`,
`kod-constitution-2016`, `left-congress-hall`, `lewica-convention-2024`,
`nik-college`, `polling-*`, `presidential-palace-2023`,
`presidential-palace-interior`, `sejm-benches`, `sejm-chamber-2024`,
`sejm-column-hall`, `sejm-government-bench`, `sejm-new-deputies-2023`,
`sejm-press-gallery`, `sejm-voting-device`) on that date.

The eighteen new entries exist to break up over-used illustrations: a single
Sejm plenary-hall photograph previously carried 45 events, and one ballot-box,
Presidential Palace and Left-congress photograph carried most of the rest of
their subject areas. Budget votes now use the chamber's voting device, club
splits the occupied chamber, committee and list business the Column Hall, and
so on.

The free-licensed photographs of Aleksander Kwaśniewski on Commons are all from
2025. His July 2020 and July 2021 appearances therefore carry a later
photograph of the same person rather than a contemporaneous one; the image
identifies who is speaking, not when.

Agency and news-site photographs (PAP and similar) are all-rights-reserved and
cannot be redistributed here, so every source below is either public domain or
carries a Creative Commons licence, including press photographs released under
one by the Chancellery of the Prime Minister, the Ministry of Finance and party
press offices.

Exception: `wajda.jpg` was supplied directly for this project. The publication
credit identifies Tomasz Urbanek / East News, but no redistribution licence was
provided with the file; confirm permission before distributing it.
